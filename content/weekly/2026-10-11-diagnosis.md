Classic Golgari Yawgmoth posted three relevant results this week: **Moorehardcore reached the Top 8 of the October 11 Modern Challenge 32**, and **NickBolas90 reached the Top 8 of the October 6 Modern Challenge 16 and then went 5-0 in the October 8 MTGO League**. These results support two different directions. Moorehardcore played conventional Golgari with 4 [[Malevolent Rumble]], 3 [[Dredger's Insight]], and 3 [[Orcish Bowmasters]]. NickBolas90 kept the [[Mole Man, Moloid Master]] / [[Ghost Quarter]] / [[Shifting Woodland]] engine and four Insights, but moved **two [[Fatal Push]] into the maindeck**.

The emerging sideboard card is **[[Arasta of the Endless Web]]**, which appeared in both NickBolas90 results. It is a green creature accessible through [[Green Sun's Zenith]] that produces 1/2 reach blockers whenever an opponent casts an instant or sorcery. Nick also moved [[Icetill Explorer]] from the main deck to the sideboard, reducing slow opening draws while preserving access to the lands engine after boarding.

The current MTGDecks 30-day Yawgmoth figure is approximately **50%**. A separate published-deck analysis by DeckSnipe covers 34 lists from September 11 to October 10 and reports **68-80-2 in 150 matches (45.9%)**. The samples use different selection methods and should not be compared mechanically. The most concerning well-sampled matchups are Esper Blink, Izzet Prowess, Izzet Affinity/Metalcraft, and Eldrazi Bloodchief Combo.

## Notes on the lists

**Moorehardcore** plays 3 [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]], 3 [[Walking Ballista]], 4 [[Agatha's Soul Cauldron]], 4 [[Malevolent Rumble]], 3 [[Dredger's Insight]], 2 [[Green Sun's Zenith]], 3 [[Orcish Bowmasters]], and 1 [[Grist, the Hunger Tide]]. The remaining creatures include four each of [[Badgermole Cub]], [[Delighted Halfling]], and [[Young Wolf]], plus [[Endurance]], [[Ouroboroid]], [[Formidable Speaker]], and [[Strangleroot Geist]]. There is no [[Chord of Calling]], Mole Man package, Shifting Woodland, or blue splash.

Moorehardcore's sideboard is 1 [[Abrupt Decay]], 2 [[Ashiok, Dream Render]], 1 [[Culling Ritual]], 2 [[Fatal Push]], **1 [[Force of Vigor]]**, 1 [[Murderous Cut]], 1 [[Outland Liberator]], 1 [[Scavenging Ooze]], 1 [[Soulless Jailer]], 2 [[Thoughtseize]], and 2 [[Vexing Bauble]]. The single Force is unusual and should not be treated as an established recommendation to cut the second copy.

**NickBolas90** uses a contrasting configuration: 3 Yawgmoth, 3 Ballista, 4 Cauldron, 3 GSZ, 2 Rumble, **4 Insight**, **2 maindeck Fatal Push**, 1 Mole Man, 1 Ghost Quarter, and 1 Shifting Woodland. Nick plays three Young Wolves and no blue splash. The sideboard is 1 [[Arasta of the Endless Web]], 1 [[Icetill Explorer]], another Ghost Quarter, 1 Abrupt Decay, 2 Ashiok, 1 Endurance, 2 Force of Vigor, 1 [[Guerrilla Gorilla]], 1 [[Pawpatch Formation]], 2 Thoughtseize, and 2 Vexing Bauble.

The older [[Breeding Pool]] / [[Consign to Memory]] package is absent. The October 6 Challenge Top 8 and October 8 League 5-0 strengthen this version's case, although they are results from the same pilot, not independent corroboration.

One data-cleaning warning: the October 8 **AwesomPossum** 5-0 sometimes appears under "Yawgmoth" on aggregation sites, but the actual list is Samwise/Birthing Ritual Combo, not classic Golgari Yawgmoth. It is excluded here.

## How Yawgmoth adapted this week

The archetype is **diverging into two successful solutions**. Moorehardcore continues the simple, interactive Rumble/Bowmasters trend from last week's fclyra 5-0. NickBolas90 instead commits to Insight and recursive lands but uses two game-one Fatal Push to address the fast creature/combo field. Both retain the stable 3-Yawgmoth / 3-Ballista / 4-Cauldron foundation.

The [[Icetill Explorer]] move is particularly meaningful. Instead of committing to both Mole Man and Icetill in game one, Nick starts Icetill in the sideboard. This preserves access to Ghost Quarter recursion after boarding while opening maindeck space for inexpensive answers. It is a more modest adaptation than rebuilding around the recent blue Consign splash.

The correct number of [[Dredger's Insight]] remains unresolved: Moorehardcore plays three, last week's fclyra trophy played two, and Nick plays four. [[Chord of Calling]] likewise is not required for success in either recent configuration.

## Decks pressuring Yawgmoth

These are current trailing MTGDecks matchup figures, expressed as Yawgmoth's win rate. The denominators matter as much as the percentages.

**Esper Blink: 33% over 21 matches.** This is the clearest established problem. Repeated exile removal and sweepers reward careful threat pacing. Preserve the ability to rebuild after the first [[Solitude]] or [[Wrath of the Skies]] sequence rather than deploying all engine pieces at once.

**Izzet Prowess: 44% over 36 matches.** The sample is fairly substantial. Nick's two maindeck Fatal Push directly address this kind of opponent; a four-mana Arasta sideboard bullet is intriguing but may be too slow against the fastest draws.

**Izzet Affinity/Metalcraft: 42% over 19 matches.** Affinity occupied both final seats in the October 6 Challenge. Two [[Force of Vigor]] remains the safer baseline, supported by [[Guerrilla Gorilla]], [[Outland Liberator]], or [[Abrupt Decay]] as needed.

**Eldrazi Bloodchief Combo: 46% over 35 matches.** Another significant below-even sample. Cheap removal and [[Thoughtseize]] should remain priorities before loading up on slow value cards.

**Amulet Titan: 33% over six matches; Ruby Storm: 17% over six; Living End: 25% over four; Neoform: 25% over eight.** These are concerning but much less reliable samples. [[Ashiok, Dream Render]], Thoughtseize, and graveyard interaction have useful overlap across these decks, so dedicated narrow cards are not yet necessary.

**Esper Reanimator is 48% over 31 matches** and looks close to even. **Boros Energy is 80% over 15**, while Eldrazi Ramp is 69% over 13, although neither percentage should be treated as a matchup guarantee.

## Sideboard cards with the strongest evidence

**[[Force of Vigor]]** is the strongest established two-of. Both copies appeared in every one of the 34 Yawgmoth lists in the September 11–October 10 published-deck sample. Moorehardcore's October 11 single copy is a new exception, not enough to change that baseline.

**[[Fatal Push]]** appeared in about 91% of those lists' sideboards. Nick's use of two copies in the maindeck is an important structural change because Prowess and Eldrazi Bloodchief are still problematic.

**[[Thoughtseize]]** appeared in roughly 82% of sideboards, normally two or three copies. It remains the broadest open-field answer to noncreature combo and control.

**[[Abrupt Decay]]** appeared in roughly 74% of lists and **[[Vexing Bauble]]** in 71%. Decay covers varied low-cost permanents; Bauble is valuable against free spells but also affects your own free interaction, so sequence appropriately.

**[[Ashiok, Dream Render]]** appeared in about 62%; [[Kraul Harpooner]] 59%; [[Culling Ritual]] 53%; [[Soulless Jailer]] 50%; and [[Outland Liberator]] 44%. These are frequency measurements in published lists, not proof that an individual card independently increases win rate.

**[[Arasta of the Endless Web]]** is the emerging test. Its 3/5 reach body and tokens against opposing instants or sorceries fit spell-heavy creature matchups, and it is accessible with GSZ for X=4. However, this costs five total mana via GSZ and its two results come from a single pilot. Treat it as an experimental sideboard one-of, not stock.

Opposing lists continue to play [[Consign to Memory]], [[Vexing Bauble]], and graveyard/tutor hate such as [[Grafdigger's Cage]]. Cage prevents creatures entering the battlefield from libraries and therefore interferes with [[Green Sun's Zenith]].

## New and newly relevant cards

**[[Arasta of the Endless Web]] is the only new Yawgmoth-specific sideboard experiment that merits a test this week.** It is an older Magic card rather than a newly printed spell, and one copy is enough as a tutor bullet. Its success remains pilot-specific.

The bigger maindeck lesson is **two [[Fatal Push]] in the lands configuration, with [[Icetill Explorer]] moved to the sideboard**. This is the most actionable technical change to try against the current Prowess/Bloodchief-heavy field.

A minor watch item is a three-copy [[Necromentia]] package in some October paper sideboards. Results so far do not establish that it outperforms Thoughtseize or Ashiok, and it does not yet warrant replacing the established sideboard core.
