**Moorehardcore produced another classic Golgari Yawgmoth 5-0 in the October 31 MTGO League**, following a 5-0 on October 25. The difference between those two trophy lists is exceptionally small but informative: the October 31 deck has **4 [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]] and 2 [[Orcish Bowmasters]]**, replacing the October 25 split of three Yawgmoths and three Bowmasters. Both use 4 [[Malevolent Rumble]], 3 [[Dredger's Insight]], 2 [[Green Sun's Zenith]], no [[Chord of Calling]], and no land-recursion or blue-splash package. Their sideboards are identical.

This is evidence that the fourth Yawgmoth is still a viable choice, **not** proof that four is optimal. Moorehardcore also finished 2-4 in an October 31 Modern Challenge. The current MTGDecks 30-day Yawgmoth win rate is approximately **45%**, and the largest unfavorable matchup samples remain Esper Blink, Izzet Affinity, Eldrazi Bloodchief, and Izzet Prowess.

There is an important classification trap this week: **Rack's October 28 League 5-0 and Challenge Top 8, and AwesomPossum's October 31 Challenge third place and League 5-0, are Samwise/Birthing Ritual builds with only one Yawgmoth.** They are not successes for the classic Cauldron/Ballista shell and should not be merged into this report's results.

## Exact comparison: Moorehardcore's two trophies

The October 31 list plays 4 [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]], 3 [[Walking Ballista]], 4 [[Agatha's Soul Cauldron]], 4 [[Badgermole Cub]], 4 [[Delighted Halfling]], 4 [[Young Wolf]], 4 [[Malevolent Rumble]], 3 [[Dredger's Insight]], 2 [[Green Sun's Zenith]], 2 [[Orcish Bowmasters]], and 1 [[Grist, the Hunger Tide]]. It includes one each of [[Endurance]], [[Formidable Speaker]], [[Ouroboroid]], and [[Strangleroot Geist]].

It has **no Chord, Mole Man, Icetill Explorer, Shifting Woodland, or Breeding Pool**. The mana base is conventional Golgari, with three [[Spymaster's Vault]] and the normal fetchland/shockland structure.

The full sideboard remains **3 [[Fatal Push]], 2 [[Force of Vigor]], 2 [[Ashiok, Dream Render]], 2 [[Thoughtseize]]**, and one each of [[Abrupt Decay]], [[Culling Ritual]], [[Endurance]], [[Outland Liberator]], [[Soulless Jailer]], and [[Vexing Bauble]].

The exact one-for-one maindeck adjustment between the two 5-0 lists is **+1 Yawgmoth / -1 Bowmasters**. There are plausible advantages on each side: a fourth Yawgmoth increases direct access to the namesake draw engine, while Bowmasters improves early interactive pressure. A pair of League trophies cannot identify the better choice, but it establishes that the fourth Yawgmoth can succeed in the contemporary shell.

## How Yawgmoth changed this week

The strongest finding is **continued success with a streamlined, no-Chord, no-splash Golgari build**, not the appearance of a new engine. Three Insights and four Rumbles remain viable together, and two GSZ is sufficient in Moorehardcore's configuration.

The fourth Yawgmoth is a genuine reversal of the recent three-copy norm. In the earlier September 25–October 24 DeckSnipe sample of classic Yawgmoth lists, only about 8% included a fourth. One trophy is not enough to infer a format-wide shift, especially as the same player also had a 2-4 Challenge finish on October 31.

Recent lands-package and blue-Consign builds did not receive new strong *classic Yawgmoth* finishes in the inspected period. Their absence from this week's benchmark does not invalidate them; there simply is no fresh evidence to upgrade them above conventional Golgari.

**Broodscale is the single largest metagame presence, at approximately 13.1% of Modern decks in the current 30-day MTGMeta sample.** Broodscale also won an October 31 Modern Challenge. Its combination of [[Basking Broodscale]], [[Blade of the Bloodchief]], colorless acceleration, and [[Urza's Saga]] rewards interaction that can hit creatures and artifacts, but the Eldrazi and big-creature backup plan makes pure combo hate insufficient. The Broodscale percentage is a metagame share, not a direct Yawgmoth-versus-Broodscale win rate. [Broodscale overview](https://mtgmeta.com/metagame/modern/broodscale); [October 31 Challenge](https://deckscout.fyi/mtgo/tournament/39501/).

For practice, prioritize **Eldrazi Bloodchief/Broodscale**, **Esper Blink**, **Izzet Affinity**, and **Izzet Prowess**. Against Blink, avoid exposing the entire engine to one sweeper. Against Prowess and creature combo, mulligan toward hands that affect the board before turn three. Against artifact decks, preserve instant-speed interaction rather than relying entirely on sorcery-speed land hate.

## Sideboard cards with the strongest current evidence

**[[Force of Vigor]]** remains the most stable artifact/enchantment answer. All 53 classic Yawgmoth lists in the September 25–October 24 DeckSnipe sample played it, and 94% used two copies. Moorehardcore continues with two. Between Broodscale and Affinity, cutting below two would require a strong local metagame reason.

**[[Fatal Push]]** is similarly supported: 92% sideboard adoption in that earlier sample, with three in 51%. Moorehardcore keeps three, and the current Prowess/Eldrazi Bloodchief matchups justify the slots.

**[[Ashiok, Dream Render]]** and **[[Thoughtseize]]** are the broadest combo-and-search package. Two of each appear in both Moorehardcore trophies. Ashiok is particularly relevant to Amulet and searching; Thoughtseize serves control and spell-based combo as well.

**[[Endurance]]** has remained reliable graveyard coverage, one main and one side. **[[Outland Liberator]]** is useful as tutorable artifact/enchantment removal; it is in Moorehardcore's sideboard but not maindeck this week. **[[Abrupt Decay]]**, **[[Culling Ritual]]**, **[[Soulless Jailer]]**, and **[[Vexing Bauble]]** round out the successful broad board.

These are **success in inclusion and repeated adoption**, not estimates of the cards' independent contribution to match win rates. The available published data does not isolate those effects. The earlier sample showed sideboard adoption of 83% for Ashiok, 87% for Thoughtseize, 89% for Endurance, and 77% for Abrupt Decay. [DeckSnipe archetype data](https://decksnipe.com/archetype/modern/golgari-yawgmoth).

## New cards and current testing priority

**There is no verified newly emerging classic Yawgmoth card this week.** The important change is a single copy-number swap between known staples: fourth Yawgmoth versus third Bowmasters. The earlier [[Arasta of the Endless Web]], [[Spore Frog]], [[Bridgeworks Battle]], and [[Consign to Memory]] experiments are not promoted by this week's best classic results.

The cleanest next test is Moorehardcore's **4 Yawgmoth / 2 Bowmasters** against the **3 Yawgmoth / 3 Bowmasters** version, keeping the other 74 cards unchanged. Test game-one hands and sideboarded games separately against the four prioritized matchups. The current evidence is insufficient to justify acquiring any new card solely for Yawgmoth.
