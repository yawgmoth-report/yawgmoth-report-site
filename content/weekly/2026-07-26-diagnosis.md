Yawgmoth had its strongest week in several reports. **Xerk** won the July 22 Modern Challenge 96 at 10-0, and **TheMeatMan** followed with a 5th-place, 7-1 finish in the July 26 Modern Challenge 64. Those are exactly the kinds of results that matter: large MTGO events, clean Cauldron/GSZ shells, and very little reliance on speculative technology.

The clearest conclusion is that **classic Cauldron/GSZ Yawgmoth is the default again**. The best lists are mostly on 4 [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]], 3 [[Walking Ballista]], 4 [[Agatha's Soul Cauldron]], 3 [[Green Sun's Zenith]], 4 [[Malevolent Rumble]], 2 [[Dredger's Insight]], 1 maindeck [[Endurance]], and no [[Chord of Calling]].

This was more a consolidation week than an innovation week. The successful classic lists are close to each other, and the differences are concentrated in one or two flex creatures and the last few sideboard slots. The experimental lands package from **NickBolas90** is still posting results, but it remains a branch to test rather than the new stock version.

## Notes on the lists

**Xerk** is the current benchmark. The July 22 winning list plays 4 [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]], 3 [[Walking Ballista]], 4 [[Agatha's Soul Cauldron]], 3 [[Green Sun's Zenith]], 4 [[Malevolent Rumble]], 2 [[Dredger's Insight]], 1 [[Endurance]], 1 [[Haywire Mite]], 1 [[Ignoble Hierarch]], 1 [[Grist, the Hunger Tide]], and no [[Chord of Calling]]. The sideboard has 3 [[Thoughtseize]], 2 [[Fatal Push]], 2 [[Force of Vigor]], and one-copy bullets including [[Ashiok, Dream Render]], [[Culling Ritual]], [[Fulminator Mage]], [[Murderous Cut]], [[Soulless Jailer]], [[Vexing Bauble]], and [[Witherbloom Charm]].

**TheMeatMan** used almost the same core on July 26. The maindeck difference is [[Virulent Emissary]] over [[Haywire Mite]]. The sideboard moved toward 3 Fatal Push, a second Endurance, [[Outland Liberator]], and a second Grist, while dropping Ashiok, Murderous Cut, and Witherbloom Charm.

**fclyra** is the middle point between those two branches. The July 25 League list uses both Haywire Mite and Virulent Emissary, with 2 Vexing Bauble, 1 [[Damping Sphere]], 1 [[Kraul Harpooner]], 3 Fatal Push, and 2 Thoughtseize in the sideboard.

**NickBolas90** continues to test the lands package. The July 23 Challenge list uses [[Mole Man, Moloid Master]], [[Icetill Explorer]], [[Shifting Woodland]], and two total [[Ghost Quarter]]. The July 22 League list keeps Mole Man and Shifting Woodland but splashes white for [[Yasharn, Implacable Earth]]. That suggests the package is still being tuned rather than solved.

**m4v3n**, **Zoru**, and **Sam_Lewin** are in the Samwise/Birthing Ritual family. These lists are successful and often appear under Yawgmoth in tournament databases, but they are structurally different from classic Golgari Yawgmoth. They use [[Birthing Ritual]], [[Chord of Calling]], [[Cauldron Familiar]], [[Gilded Goose]], [[Samwise Gamgee]], and only one copy of Yawgmoth.

## How Yawgmoth adapted this week

The deck has moved decisively toward **four Yawgmoth and no Chord**. The strongest classic lists are maximizing engine density rather than adding instant-speed tutor flexibility.

The stock main-deck flex package now looks like 1 [[Endurance]], 1 [[Formidable Speaker]], 1 [[Ouroboroid]], 1 [[Strangleroot Geist]], 1 [[Grist, the Hunger Tide]], and either [[Haywire Mite]], [[Ignoble Hierarch]], or [[Virulent Emissary]]. Xerk used Haywire Mite and Ignoble Hierarch; TheMeatMan used Ignoble Hierarch and Virulent Emissary; fclyra used Haywire Mite and Virulent Emissary.

The [[Dredger's Insight]] count has stabilized at two in the strongest MTGO lists. The high-grind versions with three or four Insights still exist, but the best event finishes this week favor two Insights plus a compact flex package.

The sideboard is also fairly stable. The normal structure is 2-3 [[Fatal Push]], 2-3 [[Thoughtseize]], 2 [[Force of Vigor]], 1-2 [[Vexing Bauble]], 1 [[Culling Ritual]], 1 graveyard permanent or Endurance, and one or two flexible creature bullets.

## Decks pressuring Yawgmoth

**Izzet Affinity / Izzet Metalcraft** remains the highest-priority bad matchup. Affinity is one of the most represented decks in the current metagame, and artifact decks keep forcing Yawgmoth to respect [[Force of Vigor]], Haywire Mite, Outland Liberator, and Culling Ritual.

**Izzet Prowess** is still dangerous. It punishes slow starts, low-interaction hands, and fetch-shock sequencing. Fatal Push remains essential.

**Ruby Storm** is the main pure-combo matchup. Yawgmoth needs disruption plus a clock; Thoughtseize, Vexing Bauble, and Damping Sphere matter, but none of them is enough if the hand does not develop quickly.

**Goryo's Vengeance and Living End** keep the maindeck Endurance trend justified. A single Endurance is rarely dead, and the sideboard should retain access to another graveyard piece when those decks are expected.

**Azorius/Jeskai Control and Blink** remain the sweepers-and-exile matchups. [[Wrath of the Skies]], [[Solitude]], countermagic, and exile removal make overextension risky.

**Boros Energy and Eldrazi Tron** are still important because they are highly represented, but they should not distort the whole sideboard plan. The pressure points this week are Izzet, Storm, graveyard combo, and white control.

## Sideboard cards overperforming

[[Thoughtseize]] is one of the strongest recurring signals. Xerk and TheMeatMan both played three copies in their best finishes. It is still the broadest card against Storm, Goryo's, control, combo, and the unknown parts of the field.

[[Fatal Push]] is settling between two and three copies. Xerk played two in the winning list; TheMeatMan and fclyra played three. The third copy is attractive when Prowess, Energy, Broodscale, and creature engines are common.

[[Force of Vigor]] remains a near-automatic two-of. The artifact decks are too common to cut lower, and the card also covers random enchantment or artifact hate pieces.

[[Endurance]] is now a maindeck default in many lists, with a second copy often appearing in the sideboard. It covers graveyard decks while still being a reasonable creature in fair games.

[[Culling Ritual]] remains the standard one-copy reset. It is good against Energy boards, artifact starts, tokens, and low-cost combo permanents.

[[Vexing Bauble]] remains important, but the best lists are not automatically maxing it. Xerk and TheMeatMan each played one copy; fclyra played two.

[[Haywire Mite]], [[Outland Liberator]], and [[Kraul Harpooner]] are the creature-bullet slots to watch. Mite is the cleanest maindeckable artifact/enchantment answer, Outland Liberator is the sturdier sideboard version, and Kraul Harpooner has continued to show up as a specific flying-threat answer.

[[Wrath of the Skies]] is the opposing card to respect most from white decks. Do not commit every engine creature into open white mana unless the board state requires it.

## New or newly relevant cards in Yawgmoth lists

There is no major new breakout card this week. The strongest finishes came from already-established cards in a tighter shell.

[[Virulent Emissary]] is the main flex creature gaining ground. TheMeatMan used it in the July 26 Challenge Top 8 and the July 25 5-2 list, while fclyra paired it with Haywire Mite in the July 25 League 5-0.

[[Haywire Mite]] remains the main competing maindeck bullet. Xerk used it in the 10-0 list, and fclyra used it together with Virulent Emissary.

[[Ignoble Hierarch]] now looks like part of the stock Xerk/TheMeatMan-style configuration. It adds acceleration while making small combat steps slightly less embarrassing.

[[Mole Man, Moloid Master]], [[Icetill Explorer]], [[Shifting Woodland]], and [[Ghost Quarter]] remain the most credible experimental package. The package has multiple results now, but it is still a branch rather than the default.

[[Yasharn, Implacable Earth]] appeared in NickBolas90's white-splash League list. It is a meaningful experiment, but the white splash is not supported by the top classic finishes yet.

[[Arnyn, Deathbloom Botanist]] and [[Voice of Victory]] appeared in Sam_Lewin's Samwise/Birthing Ritual list. Those cards belong to the hybrid branch, not to classic Golgari Yawgmoth.
