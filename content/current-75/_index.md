## Why this build

The list keeps the best-performing recent core: 4 Badgermole Cub, 4 Delighted Halfling, 4 Agatha’s Soul Cauldron, 3 Green Sun’s Zenith, and the compact Yawgmoth / Ballista / Cauldron kill structure. Adjacent successful lists from the last two weeks were split between Chord builds and more all-in Rumble/Ballista builds; I prefer Chord because it improves post-board access to Endurance, Outland Liberator, Soulless Jailer, Sephiroth, Yawgmoth, and corner-case bullets.

The sideboard is aimed at the actual current field: Fatal Push for Energy/Prowess/creature combo, Force of Vigor and Outland Liberator for Affinity/artifact engines and hate pieces, Fulminator Mage for Eldrazi/Tron/Titan-style mana, and Thoughtseize/Vexing Bauble/Endurance/Soulless Jailer for combo, stack, and graveyard decks. Magic.gg’s June 2026 Modern overview had Boros Energy, Izzet Affinity, Izzet Prowess, Grixis Reanimator, Esper Goryo’s, Eldrazi variants, Ruby Storm, and Golgari Yawgmoth all in the winner’s metagame, and specifically noted Thoughtseize and Vexing Bauble as common disruptive sideboard tools.
